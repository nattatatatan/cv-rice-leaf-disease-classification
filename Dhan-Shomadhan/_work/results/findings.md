## Composite realism check (human, blind) - T5.2

Nattan reviewed `composites_realism_check.png` (20 composites + 20 real field train images, shuffled 5x8 grid, split 0, seed 7;
composite recipe of commit d149b7a, before the white reject list existed) without the key. 12 images were flagged as composites
and all 12 were composites (precision 12/12); 8 of the 20 composites were missed (recall 12/20 = 60%), including positions 3, 11, 12
and 13. The composites are therefore identifiable more often than not. Tells reported:

- the pasted leaf does not match the scene's lighting (brightness/colour context);
- the pasted leaf is spatially detached from the plants around it, e.g. a leaf entering from the top edge (position 5);
- a visible remnant of the white paper background around the leaf (position 14).

Consequence: before Method A trains, composite placement and harmonisation are revised (T5.2 steps 3-6) and the blind check is
repeated.

Recipe v2 (decision P9, 2026-10-13) answers each tell: the leaf base sits on or below the bottom edge and the leaf leans along the
background's leaves (no leaf hangs in from the top or floats mid-frame); lightness follows the local light of the surrounding
vegetation, with the leaf's detail contrast, colourfulness and healthy colour pulled toward it; paper-coloured pixels near the mask
edge are cut and the rim recoloured from inside. A development sample of the same 20 leaf/background pairs (not the check images)
showed none of the three tells. The blind check was repeated on v2 with a new seed (`REALISM_SEED` = 2). Pass rule, stated before the check: overall
accuracy below 26/40 (the one-sided binomial p < 0.05 threshold against guessing). Result: 18 flagged, 13 of them composites
(precision 13/18 = 72%, v1 12/12), 13 of the 20 composites found (recall 65%, v1 60%), 5 real images flagged; 28/40 correct
(one-sided p = 0.008; v1 32/40). **v2 fails the rule**: composites are still told apart more often than chance, although less
cleanly than v1 (5 false alarms instead of none). Method A (E3-A) trained on v2. All evaluation uses real images only, so
detectable composites cannot inflate a reported number; they can only make the extra training samples less useful (or teach
a paste cue that real test images never trigger).

Tells Nattan reported for v2 (after the check): no longer colour or brightness, but the **pose of the leaf**. A pasted leaf is
a flat, whole leaf lying in the middle of the scene and standing out from the clump, whereas real field leaves come at varied
angles and positions, overlap their neighbours and are partly hidden. Colour matching cannot fix this: the white-background
photos are taken flat, so every pasted leaf brings its flat pose with it. Nattan's judgement is that someone not involved in
the project would find v2 realistic; the checker knew how composites are made, so the check is a strict one.

## Method A (composites) - T5.4

Recipe v2, 0.5x composites per epoch (269; the split-0 sweep gave val mixed F1 0.790 at 0.5x vs 0.773 at 1x), Swin-T, rect,
Kaggle T4 x2. Mean +- std over the 5 splits (ddof=1); paired = per-split difference.

- **E3-A vs the plain unified model (E2-swin-U), with real field images in training.** No gain. Validation: mixed 0.785 vs
  0.792 (paired -0.007 +- 0.017), field 0.765 vs 0.753 (+0.012 +- 0.099). Test, opened after gate G2 froze plain: mixed
  0.750 vs 0.763 (-0.013 +- 0.032), field 0.680 vs 0.675 (+0.005 +- 0.074), white 0.776 vs 0.798 (-0.022 +- 0.049). Test
  agrees with validation, so there is no val/test discrepancy to discuss. One visible effect: E3-A's spread over splits is
  about half of plain's (test mixed std 0.013 vs 0.033), which may be worth a sentence but is five splits only.
- **E3-A-WF vs E2-swin-WF, no real field images as training samples.** Large gain. Test field macro-F1 0.647 +- 0.084 vs
  0.300 +- 0.027 (paired +0.347 +- 0.063, ahead on 5 of 5 splits), white unchanged (0.799 vs 0.809, -0.010 +- 0.042). That
  is within 0.028 +- 0.032 of the unified model trained on real field images (0.675). Caveat for the report: field images
  are still used as composite backgrounds and their class chooses the background, so this is "white + field backgrounds",
  not pure W->F.
- **Reading.** Composites carry most of the field signal when real field leaves are absent, but add nothing once the model
  already sees about 240 real field images per split (778 training images, 538 of them white). Recipe v2 still failed the blind realism check (above), so a more
  realistic recipe might help E3-A; we did not iterate further, because repeated recipe tries scored on the same validation
  splits would make a chance win likely.

## Domain transfer questions (phase 04)

**1. Domain gap.** Field test macro-F1 is 0.539 +- 0.128 for E1-FF (ResNet50 trained on field images) and 0.141 +- 0.039 for E1-WF (trained on white images only), so training on white alone lowers field macro-F1 by 0.398 (paired E1-WF - E1-FF: -0.398 +- 0.108; E1-WF ahead in 0 of 5 splits). E2-cnx-WF (white only, ConvNeXt-T) reaches 0.442 +- 0.060. E2-swin-WF (white only, Swin-T) reaches 0.300 +- 0.027. With composites E3-A-WF reaches 0.647 +- 0.084 (paired E3-A-WF - E2-swin-WF: +0.347 +- 0.063; E3-A-WF ahead in 5 of 5 splits); its training leaves are white photos, but the backgrounds are field images, so it is not pure W->F.

**2. Does white data help field?** Field test macro-F1 is 0.687 +- 0.048 for E1-U (white + field) and 0.539 +- 0.128 for E1-FF (field only): the unified model is ahead by 0.147 (paired E1-U - E1-FF: +0.147 +- 0.159; E1-U ahead in 5 of 5 splits).

**3. Does field data hurt white?** White test macro-F1 is 0.802 +- 0.062 for E1-U (white + field) and 0.809 +- 0.037 for E1-WW (white only, the E1-WF run): the unified model is behind by 0.007 (paired E1-U - E1-WW: -0.007 +- 0.048; E1-U ahead in 3 of 5 splits).

**4. Which classes collapse under W->F?** Per-class field test F1 (mean over the 5 splits). A class counts as collapsed when its F1 is below 50% of E1-FF's for that class. The mistakes are counted in the confusion matrices added up over the 5 test splits (`confusion_<exp>_field.png`).

**E1-WF collapse is not a Mac numerics effect** (checked 2026-10-13). E1-WF ran on the M2 (MPS, fp32) and predicts almost every
field image as Leaf Scald or Sheath Blight. Its split-0 checkpoint, re-run on the CPU on the 47 field test images, gives the
same top class as the recorded MPS predictions for 46 of 47 images (CPU: 35 Sheath Blight, 9 Leaf Scald, 3 Brown Spot), so
the collapse is genuine. Hypothesis, untested: ResNet50 normalises with BatchNorm, whose running statistics come from white
photos only, while ConvNeXt-T and Swin-T use LayerNorm (per image); the shifted statistics on field images could push every
input towards the same few classes, which would fit E1-WF (0.141) being far below E2-cnx-WF (0.442) and E2-swin-WF (0.300).

## Grad-CAM shortcut check (T7.5)

Shortcut check: how much of the Grad-CAM map lies inside the hand-annotated salient leaf. 43 (image, split) pairs from 24 annotated field images (22 test, 21 validation; an image counts in every split where it was not a training image). The map explains the class the model predicted; it was computed on mps from the retrained checkpoints, whose predictions equal the reported runs' on 86 of 86 pairs checked. The leaf area share is the chance level: a uniform map would put exactly that share inside the polygon.

- Leaf area share (chance): 0.072.
- Plain (E2-swin-U): energy inside 0.244 +- 0.192 (3.4x chance).
- Method A (E3-A): energy inside 0.240 +- 0.209 (3.3x chance).
- Paired difference Method A (E3-A) - Plain (E2-swin-U): -0.004 +- 0.136; Method A (E3-A) ahead in 17 of 43 pairs.

Sub-groups of the same difference: test pairs -0.013 +- 0.092 (8 of 22); validation pairs +0.006 +- 0.173 (9 of 21); without ambiguous images +0.011 +- 0.148 (15 of 33); each image once +0.011 +- 0.138 (10 of 24). With the true class explained instead of the predicted one: Plain (E2-swin-U) 0.220 +- 0.179, Method A (E3-A) 0.217 +- 0.197, difference -0.003 +- 0.123.

Reading: Method A (E3-A) and Plain (E2-swin-U) put about the same share of their map on the leaf (per-image difference +0.011, 0.4 standard errors from zero), so this check shows no effect of the composites on where the model looks. The sign of the difference is not the same in all five subsets of the table. This is 24 images with one or two splits each, and Grad-CAM at the last stage has a very coarse grid, so read it as a direction and a size, not as proof.
